# Wishing Garden — Brand Identity Notes

Living document. Captures what we know about the brand so future design work
stays coherent. Update this as the brand evolves.

---

## Who & what

- **Business:** Wishing Garden (IG: `@wishingarden`).
- **Owner:** client of Oli's (the dev).
- **What they make:** handcrafted gemstone trinkets — keychain charms with
  raw/carved crystals (clear quartz, amethyst, rose quartz, citrine, fluorite),
  hung from gold-tone hardware, presented on deckle-edge handmade-paper cards
  with kraft accent strips embossed with a sun/star motif.
- **Adjacent product:** booth setup includes earrings, beaded jewelry stands,
  small carved gemstone figurines, holiday gift packaging in kraft + jute.
- **Tagline (her own words):** _"philosophy, flowers & handcrafted trinkets for
  the soul. welcome to wishingarden, where healing begins at home, in the
  garden of our hearts."_

## Tone

Warm. Spiritual. Handmade. Quiet, not loud. Slightly witchy/cottagecore but
never costume-y. Aspires to editorial luxury (per the client's reference set:
SSFW, Jil Sander) but the product itself is soulful and folk-craft, not
fashion-architectural — so the design must hold both: **editorial restraint
in layout, warmth and softness in materials.**

Closer reference territory: **Aesop, Officine Universelle Buly, Cire Trudon,
Anthropologie editorial, SSFW** — not Jil Sander.

Avoid: pure white (#FFF), saturated jewel tones, neons, anything cold or grey.

### Typography

- **Display serif:** something with slight quirk and warmth — `Fraunces`,
  `Cormorant Garamond`, `EB Garamond`, or `Italiana`. Avoid Didone (Bodoni etc.)
  — too cold/fashion.
- **Body sans:** quiet, low-contrast — `Inter`, `Manrope`, or a humanist sans
  like `Work Sans` or `Söhne`-alternative. Tracking slightly loose.
- **Italic:** italicized serif used sparingly for editorial pull-quotes —
  fits her poetic copy ("garden of our hearts").
- **Optional accent:** a hand-drawn or art-nouveau monogram for the wordmark.
  Her cards already have a sun/star/sigil mark — consider integrating.

### Materials & textures

Deckle-edge paper, kraft cardstock, jute twine, eucalyptus, dried statice,
raw crystal, gold-tone hardware. Surfaces: warm wood, handmade paper, soft
fabric. Subtle paper grain on backgrounds is on-brand; harsh gradients are
not.

### Motifs

- Sun / star / crescent moon (already on her packaging).
- Pressed flowers, eucalyptus sprigs, ferns.
- Crystals (raw + carved).
- Gentle astrology / talisman symbolism — handle carefully, don't go full
  occult mall-witch.

## Voice

- First-person plural and second-person — "we make / for you / our garden".
- Lowercase friendly when it fits; not aggressively lowercase like a tech
  startup.
- Poetic but grounded; avoid corporate shop-speak ("shop now", "best sellers
  ★★★★★"). Prefer: "find your charm", "today's offerings", "the collection".
- Spiritual without being preachy. "Healing", "soul", "intention" are
  on-brand if used sparingly.

## Design principles for the website

1. **Whitespace is the product.** Crystals are tiny — let them breathe.
2. **Photography first.** Every page should feel like a flat-lay magazine
   spread. Type supports the image, never competes.
3. **No e-commerce noise.** No "trending", "best sellers", review stars,
   urgency banners, sale countdowns. The buying experience should feel
   like opening a small package, not Amazon.
4. **One serif, one sans, two weights of each.** Hold the line.
5. **Slow motion.** Scroll-triggered fades over 600ms+, never bouncy or
   playful. Hover states are gentle.
6. **The cart is a "basket".** The button is "find your charm" or "shop the
   garden", not "shop now".
